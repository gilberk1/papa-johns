# Papa Johns App Concept for Glaucoma

A UX case study and coded demo from September to December 2015, created with my partner Rahel Teklegiorgis at Philadelphia University. We designed an alternative ordering experience for people living with glaucoma.

## Research

Interviews, journaling, walk-a-mile immersion, affinity clusters, a radar diagram, and personas. The research led us to a simple ordering flow, no complicated image galleries, adjustable contrast and enlargement, and an icon-based option.

## Built with

HTML, CSS, JavaScript
